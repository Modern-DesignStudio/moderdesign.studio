0106

Daytonia

Dallas, Texas

Location
Dallas, Texas
Year
2017
Size
2,500 square feet
Partners
Charles D. Bobo, architect
Scope
Photography

Built with simplicity and function in mind, this home’s clean lines and asymmetry provide privacy from the outside world while maintaining a connection to nature.

Large windows connect the three bedrooms, three bathrooms, and light-filled, open-concept living spaces to a serene backyard. During a rainstorm, the butterfly roof channels water into a cascading waterfall, an architectural feature that sets the home apart in its Little Forest Hills neighborhood.
