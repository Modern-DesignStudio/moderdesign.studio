0106

Little Forest Hills

San Leandro

Dallas, Texas

Location
Dallas, Texas
Year
2022
Size
2,000 square feet
Partners
David Rolston, landscape architect
Scope
Design and build
Photography
Leonid Furmansky

Designed with the spirit of a tiny house in mind, this three-bedroom, three-bathroom home in Little Forest Hills looks modest from the street but unfolds with a sense of grandeur that results from uninterrupted sightlines and large windows.

The design uses high-end finishes — like Italian brick veneer and Spanish tile — in restrained applications, creating a feeling of quiet luxury. Featured on the AIA Dallas Tour of Homes in 2023, the design harmonizes with the character of the surrounding neighborhood while retaining its individuality.
