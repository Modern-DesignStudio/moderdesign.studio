Photography

This home is the second iteration of a well-loved design plan that creates a flow between indoor and outdoor spaces.

Custom built for a wellness-minded client, the three-bedroom, three-and-a-half bathroom East Dallas home includes a designated home gym. Though the overall aesthetic is pared-back, luxurious finishes — like the spa-like primary bathroom and the private plunge pool in the backyard — enhance the living experience.
