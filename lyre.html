0105

Lyre

Dallas, Texas

Location
Dallas, Texas
Year
2025
Size
3,800 square feet
Partners
Cliff Welch, architect; David Rolston, landscape architect
Scope
Photography

Nestled into a landscape of mature trees and lush greenery, this four-bedroom, four-and-a-half bathroom home in Lakewood Trails feels like a refined treehouse.

The design magnifies the beauty of its setting near White Rock Lake with natural materials, like the wood and brick facade and the vertical Douglas fir siding under the roof. Large windows frame the surrounding branches, while inside, streamlined interiors and custom built-ins offer a calm contrast.
