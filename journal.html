Building beautiful experiences with Porcelanosa

In our design philosophy, details make the difference. Whether it’s the foundation, framing, paint, plumbing, or tile, we only work with vendors and subcontractors who do great work. We pride ourselves on being choosy about the people we work with and building lasting relationships with them.

That’s why we’re so pleased to have found Porcelanosa. Porcelanosa manufactures and sells ceramics, stone, and other products. But really, Porcelanosa is dedicated to experience.

Floors, walls, countertops, faucets, bathtubs, toilets, lighting, vanities—these are functional items. First and foremost, they have to accomplish their purpose. As the saying goes, form follows function. Our belief, though, is that a good life is beautiful and that a home is not functional unless it is beautiful.

That’s why we love Porcelanosa. They are dedicated to creating beautiful experiences in everything they make and do. The recently completed home at 9026 Groveland has tile, vanities, toilets, and cabinetry from them. In the past, we’ve used their faucets, showerheads, shower controls, and more. They use the best materials to create sleek, elegant products—just the way we build our homes.

Porcelanosa’s products make life a beautiful experience. They also create amazing events. They sponsored our recent open house at 9026 Groveland, and it was an incredible event. That’s why we’re so excited and grateful that they’re bringing us to Spain to tour their factory. It will surely be a beautiful experience.

Open Concept, Open House

We held an open house at 9026 Groveland last Thursday. It was such a privilege to host friends, neighbors, and prospective clients at Groveland. Getting to visit with everyone and see people enjoying themselves in a home we designed and built was so fantastic. A huge thank you goes to Porcelanosa for sponsoring the event! Everything was perfect.

Part of the design of this house that makes it great for entertaining is the open concept. The open floor plan links the kitchen and living area. Our signature floor-to-ceiling windows and numerous sliding doors take the party outside as well. Together, the kitchen, living room, and yard form an open and inviting space perfect for hosting large gatherings.

Traditional closed floor plans break up big gatherings into mini-parties. You end up with one group in the kitchen, one in the living room, another on the patio—everyone gets spread out because each space is too small to host everyone. To gather everyone, it feels like you need to rent out a country club ballroom. An open concept home makes hosting large groups easier. At the open house, the combined kitchen-living area felt like a ballroom. We had a fantastic live musician and plenty of space for people to enjoy him. There was space enough for dancing, too! Serving food and drinks was easy and the kitchen was not cramped.

The exterior wall of the kitchen-living area, as well as the master bedroom, is glass. A signature part of our design, the floor-to-ceiling windows with numerous sliding doors soften the lines between indoors and outdoors. The northeast-facing windows and glass doors provide beautiful natural light for everyday living. They also help add the yard to the party space.

To us, openness—to ideas, culture, adventures, and people—is a core part of the modern lifestyle and attitude. That’s why openness is a core part of our design philosophy. We loved sharing the Groveland home with all our friends and neighbors. Very soon, it will be a new home for a Dallas family.

Master Bedroom, Bath, and Closet at Groveland

Hallways do not spark joy.

The 9026 Groveland house is coming along beautifully. We particularly love the way the master bedroom is turning out. The windows brighten the room up, and the Douglas fir beams add a warm color. The high slanted ceiling makes it spacious and airy, and a sliding glass door gives easy access to some fresh air in the yard.

To maximize living space, we opted to put the master closet off the master bath. With this layout, you can find everything you need to get ready for your day (or get ready for bed) right in one place. To get your clothes, just go through the master bath. No space is wasted on a hallway, and no time is wasted walking between the shower and bathroom.

This modern efficiency is just one way we build our homes for your lifestyle.
