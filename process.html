Process

From first conversation to final detail.

We are personally involved in every stage of building your home. These are the four stages we move through together.

Listen

Every home starts with a conversation. We meet with you to understand how your family lives, what the site offers and what you want the home to do, then share that vision with our architects.

Design

With the architect and landscape architect we shape plans around light, views and flow between indoors and outdoors. Natural materials and energy efficiency are part of the design from the start.

Build

We hand-pick our subcontractors, select the best materials and spend our days on the job site. Being personally involved is how we get every detail right, and you are welcome to tour the site and see the work for yourself.

Move in

We finish with the same care we began with. Each home is built to be energy efficient and ENERGY STAR certified, and fitted to the life of the people who will live in it.
